Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy (Web)

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Carousel (“we”, “our”, or “the website”) handles information when you use our web application.

Data Collection

We collect minimal, anonymous data to understand website usage.

Our website is designed with privacy as a priority. We do not:

  • Collect personally identifiable information
  • Track individual user behavior across sessions
  • Store personal data on remote servers
  • Share identifiable information with third parties
  • Require user accounts or registration

Third-Party Services

Analytics

We use a third-party analytics service to collect anonymous visiting statistics. This service collects only the following information:

  • Number of page visits
  • General geographic region (country level)
  • User agent (browser type and version)
  • Referral sources (which website linked to ours, if any)

This data is collected in aggregate form and cannot be used to identify individual users. The analytics service processes data in accordance with applicable privacy regulations.

Web Fonts

Our website loads fonts from third-party font services (such as Google Fonts). When these fonts are downloaded, your browser makes a request to the font provider’s servers, which may include your IP address as part of the standard HTTP request. This is necessary for the fonts to be delivered to your browser.

These third-party font providers may have their own privacy policies regarding how they handle connection data.

Local Data Processing

All application functionality and data processing that occurs within the web app happens in your browser. Any data you create or use within the app remains in your browser’s local storage and under your control.

Children’s Privacy

Our website does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The anonymous statistics we collect cannot be used to identify individuals of any age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and will be effective immediately upon posting.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website.
